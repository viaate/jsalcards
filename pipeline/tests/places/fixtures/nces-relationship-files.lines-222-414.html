Geographic Relationship Files (50.2 MB)

User Notes

  1. Geographic codes are based on TIGER 2025.
  2. School districts in TIGER 2025 represent the 2024-2025 school year.
  3. Congressional districts represent the 119th Congress.
  4. ZIP Code Tabulation Areas (ZCTAs) in TIGER 2025 represent the 2020 Census definitions.
  5. CBSA and CSA codes are based on the U.S. Office of Management and Budget's (OMB) July 2023 metropolitan and micropolitan area components.
  6. New England City and Town Areas (NECTA) were discontinued under the 2020 standards for delineating Core Based Statistical Areas.
  7. County names and codes in Connecticut were replaced with county-equivalent entities that align with state planning regions.
  8. Urban Areas in TIGER 2025 are based on the 2020 Census Urban Area Criteria.

Geographic Relationship Files (49.7 MB)

User Notes

  1. Geographic codes are based on TIGER 2021.
  2. The school districts in TIGER 2021 represent the 2020-2021 school year.
  3. Congressional districts represent the 116th Congress.
  4. TIGER 2021 uses the U.S. Office of Management and Budget (OMB) updates to metropolitan and micropolitan delineations as of March 2020.
  5. The Census Bureau updated most legal and statistical geographies as part of 2020 Census operations, but the 2020 urban area boundaries depend on final population counts for delineation. The urban area boundaries were not available at the time of EDGE's 2021 GRF production.
  6. The ZIP Code Tabulation Areas (ZCTAs) in TIGER 2021 represent the 2020 Census definitions.

User Notes

  2. The school districts in TIGER 2020 represent the 2019-2020 school year.
  3. Congressional districts represent the 116th Congress.

Similarly, 2020 ZCTA boundaries are informed by ZIP Code areas maintained by the U.S. Postal Service and were not available at the time of EDGE's 2020 GRF production. We anticipate these layers will be updated and included in future GRF collections.
