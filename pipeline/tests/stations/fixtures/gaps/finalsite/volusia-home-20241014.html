Hurricane Milton Update

Dear Volusia County Schools Family,

It is our sincere hope that you and your loved ones stayed safe during Hurricane Milton and that our community will begin to recover as soon as possible. Our dedicated crews are visiting all school and district sites, as well as bus stops and routes, to assess each situation and ensure a safe return to school next week. Monday, Oct. 14, will still be a Student Holiday/Teacher Duty Day. There is no school for students on this day. Classes for students will resume on Tuesday, Oct. 15.  

We look forward to welcoming our students back to school and continuing to support our community's recovery. We are also extremely grateful to the many employees who worked this week to run shelters and transport and feed evacuees, as well as the many who will continue to work to prepare for our return to school next week.
