School Closings & Delays

WEDNESDAY, FEB. 22

  • Salt Lake City Schools will switch to remote learning only; all schools will be closed. All morning extracurricular activities are canceled; status of afternoon and evening extracurriculars TBD.
  • Utah State University - Tooele campus: "Classes delayed AND offices closed until 10 a.m."
  • Alpine School District announces an online learning day.
    • "A determination about after school and extracurricular activities will be made by 11am and communicated through your student’s school."
  • Salt Lake Community College: "All SLCC campuses and sites will be closed for in-person classes on Wednesday, February 22nd. Students, please check Canvas for remote learning class options."
  • Murray School District to hold remote learning day
  • Tooele County School District announces virtual learning day.
  • Jordan School District schools and offices will close; Virtual learning only
  • Granite School District to hold distance learning day on Wednesday.
    • Full details on district's website.
  • Canyons School District will switch to "remote learning" on Wednesday.
    • "While all schools will be closed for the day, students are asked to continue learning via technology. Students also may be assigned other learning opportunities by their teachers. Instructions about the day’s remote-learning activities will be sent to families by schools and individual teachers. This announcement is for one day only. Parents will be notified if remote-learning extended for additional day. The morning’s schedule extracurricular activities will be canceled unless road conditions improve enough to be able to safely hold such activities. More information will be provided on Wednesday about afternoon and evening activities."
    • Anyone who loses power or internet should contact their teacher or school, and the student should then "work on previously assigned learning activities."
  • Box Elder School District will have a delayed start. There was already a "normal Wednesday one-hour delay," so the schools will have an additional hour delay. (a two-hour delay from the usual Monday/Tuesday/Thursday/Friday start times)
  • Provo City schools will have a 2-hour delayed start Wednesday. The district said if any additional adjustments are needed during the delay, those will be communicated as soon as possible Wednesday morning by phone, text, email, district website and district social media.
  • The Juab School District is advising parents and students to prepare for a potential late start Wednesday. A final decision will be communicated by district officials by 5 a.m.
  • Utah Valley University says all campus facilities will be open Wednesday. However, if the weather makes travel unsafe, students should check with their professors to determine the status of their classes. Faculty may choose to teach remotely or in person while providing online learning options for students negatively impacted by the storm. Employees who feel unsafe to travel should communicate with their supervisors about remote work arrangements.
  • NUAMES Early College High School (campuses in Layton and Ogden) will have a two-hour delayed start. School will start at 10:00 a.m.
  • North Star Academy in Bluffdale: Two-hour delay, starting at 10:30 a.m.
  • Blessed Sacrament Catholic School in Sandy: Closed Wednesday
  • Our Lady of Lourdes School in Salt Lake City: Closed Wednesday
  • Paradigm Charter Schools in South Jordan: School canceled. "It will be a snow day and will not need to be made up as we had an extra day built into our school calendar."

TUESDAY, FEB. 21

  • The University of Utah is advising its faculty to move Tuesday evening and Wednesday classes online, and also work remotely if possible. All essential university functions will remain open, as well as the university's health care system and hospitals.
