ONLINE/DISTANCE LEARNING DAY – WEDNESDAY, FEBRUARY 22, 2023 Due to extreme weather conditions and guidance from the Governor’s Office, the Utah Department of Transportation, and the Utah Highway Patrol, all Alpine School District schools will be on an ONLINE/DISTANCE LEARNING DAY on Wednesday, February 22. Because Wednesday is an early-out day for our secondary students, it is not feasible to have a two-hour delay due to transportation needs throughout the entire district. Students should check their Google classroom or Canvas account for additional information from their teacher(s).

A determination about after school and extracurricular activities will be made by 11am and communicated through your student’s school.

Thank you for your understanding and support during this pivot to an online/distance learning day!
Winter Weather Alpine School District issues this weather ADVISORY. Working in close collaboration with the National Weather Service and local meteorologists, we are making you aware of an alert about a Winter Storm Warning impacting the Wasatch Front. In the event of a shift to an online/distance learning day, parents will be notified via the student information email system. Updates will be posted Wednesday morning around 5:30 AM on social media and the district website. Please review District Policy 1800 for more information.
