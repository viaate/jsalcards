! Important Message

  • BCPS Update on School Operations Due to Hurricane Ian


    Broward County Public Schools and District offices will be closed on Wednesday, September 28, 2022, and Thursday, September 29, 2022, due to impacts from Hurricane Ian.


    All after-school activities are cancelled today, September 27, tomorrow, September 28, and Thursday, September 29, including field trips and nighttime classes. After school care is open today; however, we ask that parents pick up their children from after school care as soon as possible. Before and after school care services will be closed on Wednesday, September 28 and Thursday, September 29.


    Watch Broward County Officials and Superintendent Dr. Vickie L. Cartwright Provide an Update on Storm Preparations.


    The District continues to work closely with emergency management officials to monitor the storm and its impact on Broward County. We will provide additional updates regarding Friday through our regular communications channels and the Broward County Public Schools social media pages on Twitter and Facebook.


    Please visit BCPS Storm Resource Center for resources on preparing for the storm, information on shelters and other important local resources.
