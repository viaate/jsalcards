! Important Message

  • Weather Update and School Closure Information


    October 8, 2024
    8 a.m. Update 


    Pinellas County Schools is working closely with Pinellas County Emergency Management to monitor the path of Hurricane Milton and the possible impact on our region.  All Pinellas County Schools and offices will now be CLOSED from Monday, October 7, through Friday, October 11. All sports and school activities scheduled for those dates are canceled.  
     
    All updates will be posted on the district's weather page, www.pcsb.org/weather, and on social media, including the district’s Facebook, X,  Instagram, and LinkedIn accounts. 



    For storm shelter and preparation information, please visit https://storm.pinellascounty.org/.
