Hurricane Ian

Based on our most recent assessments of schools, as well as no power or water, The School District of Lee County has made the decision to close all schools and District offices next week (October 3-7, 2022). Please know as we work with our partners in the Emergency Operations Center (EOC), reopening our schools is one of the top priorities. We will do so in a manner that is sensible and effective. Thank you for your continued support.
