GSCS Severe Weather Update

Today at 11:00 AM, Gulf Shores City School officials attended a Severe Weather Briefing hosted by the City of Gulf Shores. It is predicted that strong winds will not impact our immediate area until 5:00 PM this afternoon at the earliest. The anticipated rainfall in our area is expected to be in the 1"- 3" range. Based on this information provided by the experts, The school day today will operate under normal conditions with dismissal at the regular time. All Friday after-school events have been cancelled/postponed including after-school care. The Senior Night Football Game versus Daphne will be played at 2:00 PM Saturday (10/19/19) at Mickey Miller Blackwell Stadium. Senior Recognition will be held on the field at 1:30 PM. 

All Saturday school events/competitions will be held as scheduled.
