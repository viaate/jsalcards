Bend/Central-Eastern Oregon Emergency Reports for Wed. Feb. 11 - 6:47 pm
Transportation (1)
• ODOT: Valley, No. Coast - A crash on U.S. 30 at Columbia Blvd. (milepost 28.4) in St. Helens has taken out the signal cabinet causing the signal to be inoperable. An ODOT electrical crew is coming out to assess and determine what it will take to replace the cabinet and other equipment damaged. ODOT has set up advanced signs along the highway and has installed temporary stop signs at the intersection. The City of St. Helens has closed Columbia Blvd. to through traffic and has set up its own detour. The timeframe to complete the repairs could be a couple of days. Motorists should expect delays at the intersection during the morning and evening commutes. Posted: Wed. 11th, 05:26 PM
